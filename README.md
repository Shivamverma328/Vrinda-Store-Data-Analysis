# 📊 Vrinda Store Annual Sales Analysis Report 2022

## 📝 Project Overview
The **Vrinda Store Annual Report 2022** is an interactive **Microsoft Excel Data Analysis Project** designed to analyze sales performance, customer demographics, order distribution, and fulfillment status across multiple sales channels[cite: 17].

This dashboard provides actionable insights to help business owners identify sales trends, target key customer segments, and optimize channel management for maximum profitability[cite: 17].

## 🎯 Key Business Insights & Visualizations

### 1. 📈 Monthly Sales Performance (Order Vs. Sales)
- **Peak Sales Month:** March generated the highest revenue (~₹19.28 Lakhs with 2,819 orders)[cite: 11].
- **Trend:** Sales peaked in Q1 (Jan–March) and maintained a steady performance throughout the remainder of the year[cite: 11].

![Order Vs Sales](Screenshot%202026-10-13%20152050.png)

---

### 2. 👩‍💼 Gender-Wise Revenue Contribution
- **Primary Shoppers:** Women contributed **64%** of the total revenue (~₹1.35 Crore)[cite: 12].
- **Secondary Segment:** Men accounted for **36%** of total revenue (~₹76.13 Lakhs)[cite: 12].

![Sales Men vs Women](Screenshot%202026-10-13%20152105.png)

---

### 3. 📦 Order Fulfillment & Delivery Status
- **High Efficiency:** **92%** of total orders were successfully **Delivered** (28,641 orders)[cite: 13].
- **Returns & Cancellations:** Returned (3%), Cancelled (3%), and Refunded (2%) comprised the remaining 8%[cite: 13].

![Order Status](Screenshot%202026-10-13%20152117.png)

---

### 4. 🗺️ Top 5 Revenue Generating States
1. **Maharashtra:** ~₹2.99M
2. **Karnataka:** ~₹2.65M
3. **Uttar Pradesh:** ~₹2.10M
4. **Telangana:** ~₹1.71M
5. **Tamil Nadu:** ~₹1.68M

![Top 5 States](Screenshot%202026-10-13%20152134.png)

---

### 5. 👥 Customer Demographics (Age Group vs Gender)
- **Highest Buying Group:** Adult Females (Age group 30–49) form the largest customer base at **34.59%**[cite: 15].
- **Growth Segment:** Teenage Females account for **21.13%** of orders[cite: 15].

![Age Vs Gender](Screenshot%202026-10-13%20152150.png)

---

### 6. 🌐 Sales Channel Share
- **Amazon:** Dominates overall fulfillment with **35.5%** of total orders[cite: 9].
- **Myntra (23.4%)** and **Flipkart (21.6%)** represent the next largest distribution channels[cite: 9].

![Orders Channel](Screenshot%202026-10-13%20152208.png)

---

## 💡 Strategic Business Recommendations
1. **Targeted Campaigns:** Focus promotional marketing primarily on adult females in high-performing states (Maharashtra, Karnataka, UP)[cite: 14, 15].
2. **Channel Focus:** Allocate advertising budgets towards **Amazon, Myntra, and Flipkart**, which together account for over **80%** of total order volume[cite: 9].
3. **Seasonal Offers:** Launch targeted discount strategies during early Q1 to capitalize on high buying intent[cite: 11].
